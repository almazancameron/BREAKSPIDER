# All 44 assets: visual review and coverage

> **Revision 02 is the current placement recommendation.** See [the revised comp and plan](revision-02/README.md). The dense proposal below is retained as history; its placement decisions are superseded. The original visual asset inspection remains useful.

Every supplied image is accounted for below. **37 selected; 7 reserved for another composition.** Selection is based on relationships and visual weight, not whether an image can technically be enlarged. The owner's permission to upscale is reflected in the larger handheld, Miku, brooch, slime, item sprites, and archive sword.

The checkerboard reveals genuine transparency. Bounding-box measurements use alpha above 10/255 and capture a single browser-decoded frame for GIFs; they are not a complete animation envelope. The sources have no uniform padding convention. In particular, Shaymin occupies only a tiny center of its 96px canvas, while the illustrated boomerang, megaphone, and cat have substantial transparent margins. Measure the visible artwork when comparing apparent scale.

Weights below are **proposed compositional weights**, not permanent properties. A 17px source can become a bold object if enlarged enough. No asset needs recoloring to belong to Breakspider.

## Why seven are reserved

These are not rejected images. The omitted assets are visually coherent with the library, but weaker additions to this particular composition:

- **Redundant weapon:** Wit's End, after Terra Blade and Lich Bane.
- **Redundant badge:** Jet, after the two Spotlight pins and darker Sketchbook badge.
- **Redundant pose:** Starforce Omega 2, after the more useful upright pose.
- **Too many character identities:** acting figure and Terraria cultist, after the existing OC/Familiars and chosen smaller references.
- **Too much additional soft creature material:** narwhal and Shaymin, after the slime and existing Familiar.

If a future pass needs more personality, use these in another room with a meaningful anchor rather than automatically enabling them in the homepage's random layer. Keeping them off Home now gives future compositions somewhere to go.

## Transparency, scale, and animation notes

All sources were decoded successfully in Chrome. Most use transparency; none should receive a rectangular background simply to make sizing convenient. Preserve image aspect ratios, including the wide ram and low strawberry. Source-box dimensions and visible drawing dimensions are different design quantities.

The tiny League items are 17–24px high at source, so two-times display sizing produces intentionally chunky, legible pixels. Terra Blade is explicitly doubled. Starforce is doubled. The illustrated brooch and mask are reduced heavily, while Miku and the handheld become larger composition anchors relative to their neighbors. Scaling is a means of shaping hierarchy, not a uniform rule for the library.
