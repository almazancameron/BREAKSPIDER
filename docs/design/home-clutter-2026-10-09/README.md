## What is proposed

The desktop comp uses **37 of the 44 supplied images**, with **56 placements** because selected Noise emblems recur. The seven omitted images have specific composition-based reasons in the asset review; they remain useful candidates for other rooms. The comp also includes **one existing creator-owned ONFF process screenshot**, separate from the 44-image count.

The main decisions are:

- Warm, torn-looking Noise shapes at the left edge; a cooler, more interrupted bank at the right. These are unequal banks, not matching borders.
- A few collected objects around About, including a small brooch on the portrait, a boomerang at the outer corner, and a loose mask/locket cluster above Projects.
- A larger Miku/TV object tucked against Sketchbook, a darker toy-and-relic pocket below the Familiar, and a cyan group around the found map.
- Actual development material behind the file pile, with small game keepsakes and a cat perched behind its opposite corner.
- A much smaller, deliberately recomposed selection on tablet and mobile.

The prototype supplies the **grammar**: busy edges, interior pockets, mixed digital materials, and meaningful overlap. The accepted production layout supplies the actual anchors. This proposal changes neither the established content arrangement nor the Phase 1 behavior.

## Scope and evidence

All 44 images were visually reviewed on four transparency contact sheets. Tiny sprites were additionally enlarged; the three GIFs were inspected at multiple frames and their timing recorded. The current homepage was built and captured at requested widths 1920, 1600, 1440, 1221, 1024, 768, 390, and 320. Classic desktop scrollbars reduce the captured content width by 15 pixels at several sizes; the filenames name the requested viewport, not the resulting image width.

The baseline and proposed image for each size were captured from the same browser page and Familiar state. The crops are useful for judging actual-size relationships that are difficult to see in a full-page thumbnail.

The first exploration is retained as [exploration 01](exploration-01-1920.png). The final plan explains the corrections made after reviewing it. The preview files and geometry records are **design reference material**, not production interfaces or a completed clutter renderer. Frozen PNGs cannot prove keyboard behavior, live anchoring, or interaction states. Those remain implementation acceptance checks.

Temporary server and browser processes used for this review have been stopped.
